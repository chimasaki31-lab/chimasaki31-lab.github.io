基本的な使い方

録音してその場で文字にする

ホーム画面右下の「録音」ボタンをタップすると録音が始まり、話した内容がその場でリアルタイムに文字になっていきます。録音を停止すると内容が保存され、一覧から後で見返せます。

完全にオフラインで動きます

文字起こしの処理はすべてお使いの端末の中だけで行われます。インターネット接続がなくても、機内モードでも問題なくご利用いただけます。

保存・共有・書き出し

ノート詳細画面から、テキストのコピー、LINE・メールでの共有、SRT・VTT・TXT形式でのファイル書き出しができます。

データについて

保存場所

録音した音声・文字起こし結果はお使いのiPhoneの中だけに保存されます。アカウント登録は不要で、当方のサーバーに送信されることはありません。詳しくはプライバシーポリシーをご覧ください。

機種変更したいとき

本アプリは端末間の自動同期・クラウド共有機能を持ちません。残しておきたいノートは、機種変更の前にSRT/VTT/TXT形式で書き出しておくことをおすすめします。

データを消したい

アプリを削除すると、録音・文字起こし結果は端末から消去されます。書き出したファイルは、お客様が保存した場所に残ります。

Thank you for using Transcription Note. Here are answers to common questions. If yours isn't covered, feel free to email us using the address at the bottom of this page.

The basics

Recording and getting text instantly

Tap the "Record" button on the bottom right of the Home screen to start recording — what you say appears as text in real time. Stop recording to save it, and you can revisit it from the list anytime.

Fully offline

All transcription happens entirely on your device. It works fine with no internet connection, even in Airplane Mode.

Saving, sharing, exporting

From the note detail screen, you can copy the text, share it via LINE or email, or export it as an SRT, VTT, or TXT file.

About the purchase

What's free

Recording, real-time transcription, sharing, and exporting are all free with no limits. The only limit is the number of notes you can save (3 free).

One-time purchase or subscription — which to choose

To save a 4th note and beyond, choose one of the following:

  • One-time purchase, ¥800: Pay once, no further charges ever.
  • Monthly, ¥400: A lower-commitment way to try it out. Once you've paid for 2 months (¥800 total), the App automatically unlocks everything permanently — no further charges after that.

Once you've paid the 2-month total, the Settings screen will show a message letting you know. At that point you can cancel your subscription and keep everything you've unlocked. To cancel, go to the Settings app → your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

Restoring your purchase (new device or reinstall)

Tap "Restore Purchase" in the Settings screen. You'll need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Refunds

Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Please visit reportaproblem.apple.com — we're not able to process refunds ourselves.

About your data

Where it's stored

Your recorded audio and transcripts are stored only on your iPhone. No account is required, and nothing is sent to our servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Switching devices

This App has no automatic sync or cloud-sharing between devices. We recommend exporting any notes you want to keep (as SRT/VTT/TXT) before switching phones.

Deleting your data

Deleting the App removes your recordings and transcripts from your device. Any file you've exported stays wherever you saved it.
