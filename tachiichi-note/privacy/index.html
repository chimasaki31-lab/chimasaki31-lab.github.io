Thank you for using Dance Formation Maker & Choreo (the "App"). The developer of the App ("we") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. The projects you create in the App (dancers, scenes, and positions) and any music files you import are stored only on your device. They are never sent to our servers or anywhere else.

1. Information the App handles

  • The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. There is no account registration or sign-in.
  • Your project data, such as dancer names, positions, and scenes, is stored only on your device. We do not send or store it on our servers, and we cannot view or access it.
  • Music files imported for Music Sync (Premium) are copied into the App's own folder on your device and used only for playback. They are never sent anywhere.

2. Exporting files

The App can export your projects as PNG or PDF files (PDF is a Premium feature). Exporting happens only when you choose to do it, and where the file goes (email, the Files app, AirDrop, etc.) depends entirely on your actions. We are not involved and do not receive the file.

3. Data sent to outside services

Creating, editing, playing back, and exporting projects all work offline. The only feature that needs an internet connection is the in-app purchase described below.

  • Apple (App Store / In-App Purchase): Premium is offered as a one-time in-app purchase, processed by Apple and our payment platform (RevenueCat). As part of this, an identifier for your device and your purchase history are sent to RevenueCat. We never receive your credit card details or other payment information directly. This data is handled under the privacy policies of Apple and RevenueCat.

The App uses no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party sign-in services. We have no mechanism of our own for collecting or sending your information.

4. Advertising

The App shows no ads and uses no third-party ad SDKs or tracking technologies. Free users are never asked to watch ads.

5. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell or provide your information to third parties. Apart from the payment processing described in "3. Data sent to outside services", no data is sent outside your device.

6. Storing and deleting data

  • Your projects and imported music files are erased from your device when you delete the App.
  • Exported files (PNG, PDF) remain wherever you saved them, and you can delete them yourself.
  • We have no way to access the data on your device.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at any particular age group and does not collect personal information from users of any age.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page. For important changes, we may also notify you in the App.
